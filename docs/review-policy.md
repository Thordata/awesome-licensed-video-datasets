# Review Policy

## Promotion states

- `pending-confirmation`: a candidate with unresolved rights or access details.
- `verified`: the authoritative terms and relevant permissions have been checked.
- `not-verified`: the source or terms could not be confirmed.

Only `verified` entries belong in `datasets/verified/` or a public recommended
index. Commercial, application-only, gated, and public-preview datasets may be
verified when their access and usage conditions are stated clearly.

## Required evidence

Every entry must link to the dataset or publisher page, the authoritative
license or usage terms, and at least one source that supports its scale,
annotations, or access status.

## Rights review

Review download access, research use, model training, fine-tuning, commercial
use, frame extraction, derived data, and redistribution separately. A public
URL does not establish permission for any of these uses.

## Editorial review

Descriptions must remain factual. Do not turn a commercial entry into an
advertisement. Do not copy upstream media, credentials, private links, or
confidential licensing records into this repository.
